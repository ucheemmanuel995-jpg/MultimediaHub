Privacy policy

Starter text: have this reviewed before public production use.

Review required. This is a starter policy written for development. It is not legal advice and must be reviewed and adapted by a qualified professional before MultimediaHub is used publicly.

Information we collect

When you register we store your username, email address and a securely hashed password. When you upload media we store the files, titles, descriptions and visibility settings you provide. Messages sent through the contact form are stored so our team can review them.

How we use it

We use this information to operate the site: authenticating you, showing your uploads, delivering media according to its visibility, and responding to messages.

Private media

Media marked private is only delivered to its owner and site administrators.

Cookies

We use essential cookies for sign-in sessions and security (CSRF protection). We do not use advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, delete your uploads and comments at any time. To request account deletion, contact us.
